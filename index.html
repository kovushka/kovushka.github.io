Hermes Calendar

A private, owner-operated Google Calendar and Gmail integration used with a locally running Hermes assistant. This is not a public service and does not accept registrations.

What it does

  • Reads the owner’s calendars and events to help with personal scheduling.
  • Creates, updates or deletes events in calendars owned by the user, when the owner requests those actions.
  • Reads Gmail messages when the owner requests email review or assistance with scheduling.

The granted permissions are Calendar read-only, management of events in owned calendars, and Gmail read-only. The integration does not request permission to send email.

Authorization and data

Authorization happens through Google OAuth. Credentials and working records are kept on the owner’s computer, not on this website. Content used in assistant requests may be processed by the configured AI provider; local execution is not a claim of offline AI processing.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. No account data, OAuth credentials, forms, analytics or login flow are hosted on this site.

Contact

Use the application’s user-support email shown on Google’s consent screen. Public project questions can be raised at the documentation repository. Do not post private account content or credentials.
