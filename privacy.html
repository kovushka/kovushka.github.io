Privacy Policy — Hermes Calendar

This policy describes a personal integration operated by its account owner, GitHub user kovushka. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner’s Google OAuth authorization, the integration can read calendar metadata and event details, and create, edit or delete events in the owner’s calendars to carry out requested scheduling tasks. It can also read Gmail message content, headers and attachments when needed for an owner-requested email or scheduling task. Gmail access is read-only.

Processing and storage

The integration executes on the owner’s computer. OAuth credentials, local working files and task history are stored locally and are not published to this website or its repository. Locally stored copies remain until the owner deletes them; the integration does not impose a universal automatic retention period.

When the owner requests help through an AI assistant, relevant selected content and tool results may be sent to the configured AI provider and retained in assistant conversation history. AI processing is not necessarily offline. The applicable provider and account settings govern provider-side retention. The integration does not use Google user data to build, train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing and limited use

Google account data is used for the owner’s requested functionality, not sold or used for advertising. It is not published on GitHub Pages. Transfers are limited to those needed for the requested functionality, including the owner’s configured AI processing service. The integration does not provide unrelated third parties with access to Google account content.

Hermes Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can stop the integration and revoke its access in Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously saved copies. The owner can delete local credentials and working files separately. Calendar events and Gmail messages remain in Google until the owner deletes them there. AI conversation history and provider-retained copies must be managed through the relevant provider.

This website

This static documentation site has no forms, analytics scripts or account-data processing. GitHub provides hosting and may collect technical request information under its own privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Contact the owner through the support email shown on the Google consent screen. Non-sensitive documentation questions can be raised in the linked GitHub repository. Do not submit credentials or personal content publicly. Updates to this policy are recorded in the repository’s version history.
